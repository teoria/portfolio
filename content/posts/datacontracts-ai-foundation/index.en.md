Write this one down:

- Create contracts for your APIs and datasets.
- Create the usage agreements.
- That's your source of truth now.

Always generate from the contracts. Always validate from the contracts. Always monitor from the contracts.

Now that your foundation is solid and reliable, use your favorite AI to build on top of it. Magically, most common errors disappear!

Naming, semantic layer, dbt models, Pydantic, catalog, REST API, frontend… everything!
