If your day-to-day is listening to the C-level and building them a dashboard in the company's BI tool… this post is for you!

Start doing exactly what you do now with AI. Use it a lot. I said A LOT.

Deliver faster and use the time to go deeper into the business: how the company makes money and how it can lose less.

In the very near future, AI will understand stakeholders' needs well enough to build their dashboards much faster than you.

If you work in analytics or BI and haven't brought AI into your daily process yet, I strongly recommend starting TODAY. The time to adapt is now.
