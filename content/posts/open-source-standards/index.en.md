When you adopt an open source standard, it's like adding the community's development capacity to your team.

Data Contract CLI has 169 contributors and 1,000 stars!

If you decide to create your own standard, you throw away 169 people who would maintain it and add new features to your tool for FREE.

Think about it… =D

"But the standard doesn't have every field my company needs." My friend, they thought about that from day one. Of course it's extensible with new fields.

Don't reinvent the wheel. Don't say I didn't warn you…
